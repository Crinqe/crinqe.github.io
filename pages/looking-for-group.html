Looking for Group

About the Site

Looking for Group is a website for the RIT community to discover and collaborate on ongoing projects. RIT students often express that it's difficult to find team members to create projects with. Looking for Group aims to fill that need by allowing developers and designers to find each other more easily.

What We Created

  • During the Summer of 2026, the team's goal was to polish the site's front end as we prepared it for launch in the Fall, which includes fixing visual bugs, improving responsiveness, creating Terms of Service, and adding moderation capabilities. The goal of the backend was to ensure that users could signup and login with their RIT accounts using Google Oauth, hook up custom image uploading, and managing user relations behind the scenes.
