Clothes It

About the Project

A utility app that allows users to try on and plan outfits virtually! This was submitted for WiCHacks 2026.

What We Created

  • Designed a utility app that addresses the issue of trying on and planning outfits physically, which takes away time and energy from the user.
  • Aimed to provide numerous customization features, such as functional color wheels that provide varying clothes, hair, and skin tone colors.
  • Designed a game that addresses the gap of realistic dress-up games, as most dress-up games tend to have fantasy themes and are unable to provide real-world applications for users.

Individual Contributions
