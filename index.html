About Me

My name is Morgan Meys, and I'm a computing student at Rochester Institute of Technology in the Game Design and Development program. I specialize in Front End Web Development, UI/UX Design, and 2D Character and Environment Art. I am also exploring an interest in 3D Art. My goal is to use computing and design principles to create visually stunning and functional programs that provide helpful services to users.
